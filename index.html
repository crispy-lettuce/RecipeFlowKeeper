Settings

How the app behaves, and the words it treats as the same thing.

INGREDIENT LOOKUP

Type an ingredient as a recipe would write it, to see what the shopping list calls it, where it goes and why, and what you have already told the app about it.

WORD MATCHES

Things you've told the app are the same. Ingredient matches get added together on the shopping list; source matches stop you being asked about a spelling you've already settled.

SHOPPING AISLES

Move something the shopping list puts in the wrong aisle. It applies to every recipe that uses the name. To total two names as one, match them in Word Matches first, then set one aisle here.

Not available yet: the aisle_overrides table has not been created on the database (docs/migrations/add-aisle-overrides.md).

SWAPS

Your own ingredient substitutions, built up over time. Each carries a scaling ratio. A swap applies wherever the shopping list would call an ingredient by the swap's name, so "butter" never catches "peanut butter".

DICTIONARY

The names and aisles the app knows by itself, each with the wordings it reads as that name. The lookup box above filters it. Changing a row is a code change; ask for it in a session.

LIBRARY CHECK

Every recipe, with what is known about its match to the page it came from and the names it puts in Other, those needing attention first. Nothing is changed here: OPEN a recipe to compare it with its source and save, and this list is the record.

SOURCES

Every distinct source currently in use. If two entries are really the same place, merge one into the other — every recipe using it gets renamed. This can't be undone. (Merging renames the recipes you already have; a word match above only stops you being asked again.)

KEYWORDS

Every keyword you can tag a recipe with. Merge duplicates, or delete one to remove it from every recipe carrying it.

RECIPE PHOTOS

Photos are copied into your own storage automatically when you save a recipe. These are for the times that cannot cover: after restoring a backup, or after saving while offline.

WEEK STARTS ON

Your planner and shopping list group days into weeks that start on this day. Friday suits a Friday shop.

APPEARANCE

Follow the system and the app changes when your device does, which on most tablets means it goes dark in the evening. Light and dark override that. The choice is shared with the household, like every other setting here.
