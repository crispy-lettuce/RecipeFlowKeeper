What it is

RecipeWrangler is a personal recipe, meal-planning and shopping-list app run privately for a few households of one family. It is not a commercial service, costs nothing, and is not offered to the public: signing in needs an account set up by the family.

Using it

Use it for your own cooking and planning, and do not try to reach other people's data or interfere with the service. The family may change, suspend or stop the app at any time.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. When connected, the app adds, updates and removes events only in its own "RecipeWrangler" calendar, for the days you switch on. You can disconnect at any time in Settings or from your Google account; the calendar and its events stay in your account. How data is handled is set out in the privacy policy.
