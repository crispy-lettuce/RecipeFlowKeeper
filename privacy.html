RecipeWrangler privacy policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

RecipeWrangler is a private recipe and meal-planning app used by a few households of one family; each sees only its own data, plus any recipes another household chooses to share with it. If you connect Google Calendar, it asks Google only for your account's email address (to show which account is connected) and for permission to manage a calendar it creates itself, called "RecipeWrangler"; it cannot see or change any other calendar or anything else in your Google account. It uses that permission only to add, update and remove all-day events for the meals you choose to put in the calendar, with an optional reminder the evening before. The Google access token is kept encrypted in the app's own database, is never shared with or sold to anyone, and is not used for advertising, analytics or training AI models; your recipes and meal plans likewise stay in the app's private database and are shared with no one, except that a household may choose to let another household of the family browse its recipes. RecipeWrangler's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can disconnect at any time with DISCONNECT in the app's Settings, or by removing RecipeWrangler at myaccount.google.com/permissions; either way the token is deleted, and the RecipeWrangler calendar stays in your Google account for you to keep or delete. Questions can be raised on the app's GitHub page.
